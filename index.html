How to print
  1. Load up to 20 blank uncoated cards in the rear tray, portrait, with the guide snug.
  2. Tap Print / Share on a part, then Print. Pick the G620, paper size Square 3.5×3.5" (89×89 mm), borderless if offered, and the heaviest paper type listed.
  3. From a Windows laptop: tap Save, open the PDF in Acrobat and use the MTG Card preset at Actual size.
  4. Backs: let fronts dry, flip the stack sideways like a book page, reload, print the BACKS file.
